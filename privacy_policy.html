RxSafety - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 | Last Updated: October 29, 2026

1. Overview

RxSafety is committed to protecting patient privacy and medical data confidentiality. This privacy policy explains how data is handled within the RxSafety application.

2. Data Storage & Local Persistence

Your medication schedule, daily adherence records, and patient profiles created within RxSafety are stored locally on your device using an encrypted, embedded SQLite database managed via Android Room. We do not transmit or sell your personal health records or medication history to external advertising networks or commercial third parties.

3. External Government API Data Queries

To provide real-time clinical screening and safety warnings, RxSafety queries public domain government APIs hosted by the United States Government:

  • NIH RxNav / RxNorm: Used to convert drug names to clinical concept identifiers (RxCUI) and evaluate drug-drug interactions. Queries transmit non-personally identifiable drug concept names.
  • US FDA OpenFDA: Used to fetch official drug monographs, boxed warnings, and live drug enforcement recall notices. Queries transmit drug brand and generic names.

4. Device Permissions

RxSafety requests minimal Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required strictly to fetch public clinical drug data from NIH RxNav and US FDA OpenFDA web services.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used strictly to deliver local medication reminders configured by the user via WorkManager.

5. Medical Disclaimer

RxSafety is an educational and clinical decision-support screening tool. It does not provide professional medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice. Always consult a licensed healthcare practitioner or pharmacist regarding medication regimen changes or health concerns.

6. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy or application safety, please contact our support team at: support@rxsafety-app.org
